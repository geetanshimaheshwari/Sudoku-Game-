How to Play ✦
1
The Goal: Fill every row, column, and 3×3 box with the numbers 1–9. No repeats allowed in any row, column, or box!
2
Select a cell: Click any empty cell to select it. Related cells (same row, column & box) will highlight in soft purple.
3
Enter a number: Click a number on the pad below the board — or press 1–9 on your keyboard. Correct answers glow purple; mistakes show in pink.
4
Notes mode ✏️: Toggle notes mode to jot down candidate numbers in tiny pencil marks inside a cell. Great for tricky spots!
5
Mistakes: You get 3 mistakes. After that it's game over — so think carefully before you place! 💕
6
Scoring: Earn points for every correct number. Finish faster for a time bonus! Hints cost 50 pts and mistakes cost 25 pts.
